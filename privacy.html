Privacy Policy

Trudia is a local-first point-of-sale system designed for offline use.

Data Storage

All restaurant data (orders, customers, sales, reports) is stored locally on your device. We do not host or access your database on any external server.

Data Collection

The application itself does not collect personal or business data. The only data submitted externally is feedback you voluntarily send through the website form.

What We Don't Do

  • • We do not track user activity
  • • We do not sell or share data
  • • We do not store your POS database online

Updates

This policy may be updated if cloud features are introduced in the future.
